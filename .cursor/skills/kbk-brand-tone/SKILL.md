# KBK Community Closet brand tone

## Mission

At KBK Community Closet, we believe every child deserves to feel seen, valued, and fully themselves. We create a welcoming, joyful space where families can share and find free, gently used children’s clothing—creating a ripple of kindness in our community.

## The voice

Fun, easygoing, and full of heart. Keep things light and approachable so families feel seen and included. Celebrate everyday moments, encourage connection, and bring people together with a smile.

## Values in action

- **Family-friendly**: Every post and event is easy for all ages.
- **Inclusive**: We welcome everyone, no matter where they’re at.
- **Community-driven**: Every action strengthens ties and lifts each other up.

## How to write it

1. **Talk like a neighbor.** Use “we” and “you,” contractions, and plain words. Short sentences that read easily on a phone.
2. **Lead with joy, not need.** Frame everything as sharing and celebrating together, never as charity or handouts.
3. **Make everyone feel invited.** Say who’s welcome (everyone) before any conditions. Donations are appreciated but never required to attend.
4. **Be a little playful.** A fun word trio or a light twist is on-brand (“spooky, silly, or simply magical”). One per piece is plenty.
5. **Be clear about the practical stuff.** Date, time, place, and what to bring should be easy to find, even in a playful post.
6. **Close with warmth.** End with an invitation or a smile: “We can’t wait to see you!”

## Word choices

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| families, neighbors, our community | the needy, less fortunate, clients, recipients |
| share, find what fits, take what you need | handouts, charity, free stuff |
| gently used, kids’ clothes | used, secondhand junk, cast-offs |
| everyone is welcome | eligible, qualified (in public copy) |
| donate, bring what’s extra | dump, get rid of |

## Facts to get right

- **Name**: “KBK Community Closet” on first mention; “KBK CC” only where space is tight.
- **What we share**: free, gently used children’s clothing, shoes, accessories, and baby gear. Maternity clothing when available.
- **We do not accept toys.** Never list toys as something we collect or give out.
- **Where**: Kennebunk, Maine. Drop-off bin and pop-ups at Kennebunk Bible Church, 71 Portland Rd.
- **Events**: donating earns early access at pop-ups; everyone is welcome during open hours.

## House style

- Curly apostrophes and quotes (’ “ ”), en dash for ranges: “4:30–5:30 PM”, “Nov 13–14”.
- Times are Eastern; write “4:30–5:30 PM” (no “:00” needed for whole hours, e.g. “5:30–7 PM”).
- Exclamation points: one or two per piece, not every sentence.

## Examples

**Social post (reference tone):**

> Come join us in the only way we know how—celebrating community! This weekend, we’re hosting a Halloween Costume Share event. Stop by and see if there’s a costume that fits your vibe—whether it’s spooky, silly, or simply magical. We can’t wait to celebrate with you!

**Rewrite: off-brand to on-brand**

Before:
> Donors who have contributed qualifying items are eligible for early entry to the distribution event.

After:
> Shared some gently used kids’ clothes with us? Thank you! Come shop early before doors open to everyone.

## Review checklist

When reviewing or finishing copy, check:

- [ ] Sounds warm and conversational, like a friendly neighbor
- [ ] Everyone feels welcome; no “need”-based or charity framing
- [ ] Practical details (when, where, what to bring) are clear
- [ ] No mention of accepting or giving out toys
- [ ] Name, location, and times match the facts above
- [ ] Ends with a warm invitation
